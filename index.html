Tips for Better AI Video Generation

  • Be specific: Include details about camera angles, lighting, and style
  • Use reference images: Many platforms support image-to-video
  • Start simple: Begin with basic prompts and refine
  • Consider motion: Specify camera movements and object actions
  • Batch generate: Create multiple variations to choose from
